Goal 8

Promote sustained, inclusive and sustainable economic growth, full and productive employment and decent work for all

What is Goal 8?

Goal 8 is one of the 17 Sustainable Development Goals set by the United Nations. It aims for an economy where everyone who wants to work can find a good job, with fair pay and safe conditions.

It also asks countries to grow their economies in ways that include everyone and do not harm the environment.

Goal 8 in short

  • Global unemployment was 4.9% in 2025.
  • About 58% of workers have informal jobs.
  • 138 million children were in child labour in 2024.
