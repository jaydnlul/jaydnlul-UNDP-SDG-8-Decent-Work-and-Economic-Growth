Goal 8 by the numbers

Use the buttons to filter the facts by topic. Figures are from the UN SDG 8 progress report.

4.9%

Global unemployment rate in 2025, close to a record low.

57.9%

Share of workers in informal employment in 2025, often without social protection.

20.0%

Young people not in employment, education or training in 2025.

138 million

Children in child labour in 2024, about 8% of children worldwide.

1.9%

Expected yearly growth of global real GDP per capita, according to UN estimates.

3.5%

Tourism's direct share of global GDP in 2024.

79%

Adults with a bank or mobile money account in 2024, up from 74% in 2021.
