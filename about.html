About this project

This website was built for PROG2015 at the University of Trinidad and Tobago. It aims to raise awareness of Sustainable Development Goal 8 among students and young people who are preparing to enter the workforce.

All facts and images come from the United Nations Department of Economic and Social Affairs.

Visit the official Goal 8 page

Some Goal 8 targets

  • 8.1 Sustain economic growth per person.
  • 8.3 Support jobs, small businesses and creativity.
  • 8.5 Full employment and equal pay for work of equal value.
  • 8.6 Reduce the number of youth not in work or school.
  • 8.7 End forced labour and child labour.
  • 8.8 Protect labour rights and safe workplaces.
  • 8.10 Expand access to banking and financial services.
